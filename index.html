01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Two engineering challenges.
One connected vision.

EV_power brings together sustainable electric propulsion and intelligent autonomous mobility. The project integrates a full-scale electric vehicle platform with a compact autonomous kit used to validate navigation software in a controlled environment.

The approach separates vehicle powertrain validation from early autonomous algorithm testing—reducing development cost and risk while creating a foundation for future integration.

A

Clean propulsion

Electric drive, energy storage and mechanical power distribution.

B

Safe autonomy

Mapping, localization and obstacle avoidance on a small-scale kit.

C

Engineering validation

Component integration, empirical checks and documented results.

01

Electric drive

A 48V, 1200W BLDC motor is connected to a matching motor controller and mechanical differential to deliver propulsion and distribute power.

02

Energy storage

The 48V 100Ah battery pack supplies the powertrain. The system is wired and verified with attention to electrical loading and safe isolation.

03

Chassis & suspension

The selected chassis model was evaluated and adapted. Suspension components were installed and examined under the vehicle's real load.
